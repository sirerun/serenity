The mark

The selected Book Binder artwork carries the Serenity identity. Keep its silhouette intact at small sizes. Use the rose mark on charcoal; choose a light or monochrome treatment when the application requires it.

Original PNG markWordmark · raster mark in SVGLight treatment · raster in SVGMonochrome treatment · raster in SVG

The original artwork is a PNG. Historic SVG paths remain as containers around that raster; the light and monochrome versions apply presentation filters and are not vector tracings.
