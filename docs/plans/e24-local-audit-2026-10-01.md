## Result

The exact local acceptance criteria are supported for T24.4–9, T24.14–18, T24.20, T24.24, and T24.26. T24.28 is incomplete in the final tree: the executable ACL implementation and its test were intentionally reverted. T24.30 is incomplete: the drift scanner covers local product packages only, while the contract requires the whole module.

There is also an erasure-scope discrepancy: T24.21's stated worktree/index criteria are tested, but ordinary source tombstones retain old source blobs in Git history. ADR 019 §1 explicitly requires source tombstones to rewrite history too. This is beyond T24.21's current test assertion and remains an E24 product gap.
