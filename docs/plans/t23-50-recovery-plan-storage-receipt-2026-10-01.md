Source base: main337 `06e22d019dccd04963f6309c13e0b45f441c7e8a`. Final candidate source: `a085f431ebe9a8971bdffd78290f7f4ef4d9ee60` (includes `1dd2acdcceb0265fb2c08d054aec4ecc5ed7fffa` and `35f4b85fe8b4f1a3b781d6b194cfec6e2fc66eb0`).

This bounded prerequisite adds `internal/hosted/recovery` plan persistence only. `CreatePlan(ctx, dir, input)` canonicalizes a sorted, unique account scope and persists an immutable, hash-named JSON artifact. Its SHA-256 covers canonical payload fields excluding the hash: format version, supplied exact manifest-byte SHA-256, deletion-journal watermark, fence generation, UTC provider observation time, and account IDs. `LoadPlan(ctx, dir, expectedHash)` reloads only that approved hash and validates the hash, canonical bytes, field relationships, and account scope. The decoder bounds file/account count and rejects duplicate keys, unknown or case-aliased fields, malformed nested objects, and trailing JSON. Plan directories must be absolute, private mode 0700, current-user owned, non-symlink directories; every ancestor is checked for trusted ownership, unsafe writable mode, and filesystem ownership enforcement. Plan files are opened without following symlinks or blocking on special files, then required to be private regular files. Publication uses a synced same-directory temporary file, atomic no-overwrite hard link, and directory sync. Existing byte-identical plans are idempotent; corrupt or conflicting paths are never replaced. Platforms without the required safe filesystem support fail closed.
