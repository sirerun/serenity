The contract explicitly says the factory publishes neither component until both token and final tuple checks succeed. No identity getter or path-only fallback is needed.

The whole recovery store and production factory remain separately unqualified; this CLEAR does not grant READY, recovery effects, provider access, restore acceptance, or deployment authority.
