## T24.20: Facts record their writer; forget is restricted to the writer or a human; memory:forget scope

Findings: AI-L03. Plan row: `docs/plans/deep-review-001-remediation.md`.

### Objective

A reviewer sees that a write-scope client cannot delete another client's shared facts: remember records the writing principal in the fact's meta, forget requires the caller to be that writer or a human actor, and a separate `memory:forget` scope exists so credentials can be issued without it. The hosted scope enforcement in the gateway is carried by the T23.45 amendment.

- internal/hosted/gateway (R-hosted-runtime; T23.45 adds the scope check).
