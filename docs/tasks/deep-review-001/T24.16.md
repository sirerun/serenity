## T24.16: Connector-kind trust: first-seen machine claims from untrusted connectors wait in the inbox; prompt carries actor and trust

Findings: AI-01. Plan row: `docs/plans/deep-review-001-remediation.md`.

### Objective

A reviewer sees that content from an untrusted connector cannot become a cited fact without a human accept: connectors carry `trust` (imap and git_repo default untrusted, file trusted); a first-seen non-conflicting machine claim from an untrusted connector is written pending and appears as a `claim_candidate` inbox item; accepting it activates the claim with the human as actor; the composer prompt and citations show actor and trust for every claim.
